Two ways to play

Classic builds a line.
Party keeps switching.

Pick a mode before you start. Classic is a slow, tense build. Party throws a different kind of turn at the whole table every round.

Classic

Build your own misery line

You start with one situation. Every turn a new one arrives without a score. Put it in the right spot in your line and it stays there — one point. Then slide the score close enough to the real one for a second point. The margin shrinks as your line grows: 5 points at first, 3 from five cards, 1 from seven.

First to the target score wins
Party

Same turn, everyone, every round

Three kinds of turn rotate. Within a round everyone plays the same one, so nobody gets an easier deal than the person next to them. After the last round, most points wins.

Set 5, 10 or 15 rounds

Questions

Reasonable concerns

How many people do I need?

Anywhere from one to eight. Solo works as a personal best run; three or more is where the arguing starts.

Do I need internet?

No. Every card ships inside the app, so the whole game runs offline once it is installed.

Where do the scores come from?

Each situation has a fixed misery index between 1 and 100, set by the deck. You are not voting on it — you are trying to work out what it already is. That is the part people shout about.

Do cards repeat?

The app remembers which cards this phone has already seen and deals fresh ones first. When the deck is exhausted, it starts over.

What do I get for free?

A starter deck of 200 cards with an average misery of around 50, both game modes, all eight languages and up to eight players. Ads appear between games.

What does the full game add?

One payment of €4.99 unlocks the complete deck and removes all ads. You buy it inside the app, there is no subscription, and you can restore it if you switch phones.

Is there an iPhone version?

Android first. Ask loudly enough and iOS follows.
